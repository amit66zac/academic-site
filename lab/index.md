<section class="lab-hero-panel" aria-labelledby="lab-mission-title">
  <p class="lab-kicker">Computational research for public-interest regulation</p>
  <h2 id="lab-mission-title">Building new ways to study law as it operates in digital markets.</h2>
  <p>
    The lab will bring together researchers from computer science, data science, law, economics,
    and behavioural research. We develop computational tools and empirical evidence that make
    large-scale digital systems more observable—and regulation more enforceable.

<section class="lab-section" id="research-programmes" aria-labelledby="programmes-title">
  <div class="lab-section-heading">
    <p class="lab-kicker">Research programmes</p>
    <h2 id="programmes-title">Two connected projects</h2>
  </div>

  <div class="lab-program-grid">
    <article class="lab-project-card lab-project-card--erc">
      <p class="lab-project-label">ERC Starting Grant project</p>
      <h3>Lex-Luthor</h3>
      <p>
        Lex-Luthor addresses the enforcement gap in digital-market regulation. It develops scalable
        ways to detect non-compliance with privacy, consumer, and competition law, comparing what
        firms say in policies and interfaces with what their digital systems actually do.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article class="lab-project-card">
      <p class="lab-project-label">Companion research project</p>
      <h3>Artificial Advice</h3>
      <p>
        Artificial Advice studies how conversational AI affects choice, visibility, and equal
        treatment. It combines behavioural experiments with automated audits to examine
        self-preferencing, manipulation, reduced diversity, and persona-specific steering.
      </p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="lab-section" aria-labelledby="methods-title">
  <div class="lab-section-heading">
    <p class="lab-kicker">Methods</p>
    <h2 id="methods-title">Research across disciplines and scales</h2>
  </div>

  <div class="lab-methods-grid">
    <article class="lab-method">
      <h3>Web measurement</h3>
      <p>Browser automation, large-scale data collection, and audits of live digital systems.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="lab-method">
      <h3>Machine learning and language</h3>
      <p>NLP, LLM evaluation, text analysis, and computational classification.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="lab-method">
      <h3>Experiments and inference</h3>
      <p>Behavioural experiments, statistical analysis, and causal research designs.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="lab-method">
      <h3>Legal-economic analysis</h3>
      <p>Translating technical evidence into meaningful indicators for law and policy.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="lab-section lab-values" aria-labelledby="culture-title">
  <div class="lab-section-heading">
    <p class="lab-kicker">How we work</p>
    <h2 id="culture-title">One collaborative research environment</h2>
  </div>
  <p>
    The lab is being established at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem as a large interdisciplinary
    group whose members design and conduct research together. Our work will emphasize ethical,

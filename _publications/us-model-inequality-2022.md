abstract: "To what extent does the choice of competition law model correlate with economic inequality? This article utilizes a comparative legal approach and a unique estimation framework based on textual similarity to estimate the differences between the US and EU models and provides evidence that countries that adopt a US-style antitrust model are more likely to exhibit higher income inequality levels over time."

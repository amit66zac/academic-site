abstract: "Rising economic inequality presents society with unprecedented challenges. In this article we explore whether competition law can play a role in reducing the widening inequality gap. We outline the possible relationship between competition law enforcement, market power, and economic inequality, supplement the theoretical discussion with empirical analysis, and emphasize the role of labour compensation as a key mechanism linking competition law enforcement, competition dynamics, and economic inequality."

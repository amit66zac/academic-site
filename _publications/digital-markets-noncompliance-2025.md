abstract: "Regulatory oversight in digital markets poses a significant challenge. Using European privacy law as a case study—and focusing on the fallout from the European Court of Justice's 2020 invalidation of the EU-US Privacy Shield—we examine how over 2,500 apps from the Spanish Google Play Store responded to this legal shift. We find a striking divergence between firms' stated privacy policies and their actual cross-border data transfer behaviors. Drawing on tools from IT security research to observe data flows typically inaccessible to legal scholars, we show that traditional policy-based methods may miss a substantial share of violations."

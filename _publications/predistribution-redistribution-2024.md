abstract: "After decades of post-WWII prosperity, economic inequality levels have been increasing in many parts of the world. This chapter examines the complex interlinks between effective competition law, intense competition, and economic inequality, arguing that competition law operates as a pre-distributive tool—shaping market outcomes before redistribution—rather than merely alleviating poverty after the fact."

abstract: "We present the first general, automated, large-scale analysis of cookie notice compliance. Our method interacts with cookie notices, observes declared processing purposes and available consent options using Natural Language Processing, and compares them to the actual use of cookies. We provide a general view of the overall compliance picture using a set of 97k websites popular in the EU. We report that 65.4% of websites offering a cookie rejection option likely collect user data despite explicit negative consent."

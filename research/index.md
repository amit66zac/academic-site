My research sits at the intersection of **competition law**, **law and economics**, and **digital markets**. I am particularly interested in:

- **Competition law and economic inequality** — how antitrust enforcement and policy design affect household inequality and the labour share.
- **Digital markets and regulation** — compliance, dark patterns, and the role of technology in enforcement (e.g. automated analysis of cookie notices, algorithmic consumers).
- **Empirical and computational methods** — quantitative and AI-assisted approaches to legal and policy questions.
- **Automation of Law** — ML-assisted approaches to legal enforcment questions.
I am leading a new ERC project **Lex Imperfecta** which uses machine learning tools to study and optimize digital markets regulation. I have also worked on projects funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) and the Leverhulme Trust.
